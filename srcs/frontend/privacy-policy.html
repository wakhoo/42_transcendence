1. Introduction

DrawDraw is a real-time multiplayer drawing-and-guessing game with chat, profiles, and friends, built as the final project of the 42 School Common Core. We are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable data protection laws.

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and what rights you have regarding your data.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data under the following legal bases (GDPR Article 6):

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)) — processing necessary to provide the service you signed up for.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — optional features such as Google OAuth login and 2FA.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — security logging and abuse prevention.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — compliance with applicable laws.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate your identity, including two-factor authentication (2FA) if enabled
  • Display your profile, stats, and match history to other users
  • Enable real-time gameplay, drawing rounds, and chat
  • Maintain your friends list and online status
  • Maintain security and prevent unauthorized access

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Security Measures

The following controls protect your data:

  • Passwords hashed with bcrypt (cost factor 12) — never stored in plain text
  • All connections encrypted via HTTPS / TLS 1.3
  • 4-layer Docker network isolation — the database is never exposed externally and is only reachable from the backend
  • Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) available via TOTP
  • Email-based one-time verification codes required before sensitive changes (email/username, password, 2FA, account deletion) on accounts with no password or 2FA set up
  • JWT access tokens with short expiry, plus refresh tokens for session renewal
  • Failed login attempts are rate-limited
  • Web Application Firewall (ModSecurity with the OWASP Core Rule Set) at the reverse proxy
  • HashiCorp Vault for centralized secrets management
